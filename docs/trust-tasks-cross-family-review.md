## Interpretation

An internally consistent audit log is not necessarily externally authentic. Capability advertisements are not principal authorization. Privileged decisions require exact payload, prior state and currently authorized approvers. A consent notification is not the actual single-use grant. Temporal and pagination semantics must be checked against the exact version.

The [machine-readable research records](https://github.com/qbf-consulting/digital-trust-failure-corpus/blob/main/research/external-sources/trust-tasks/additional-screenings.json) retain source blobs, candidate classifications and negative tests. All decisions remain preliminary; source-version differences do not authorize automatic reuse.
