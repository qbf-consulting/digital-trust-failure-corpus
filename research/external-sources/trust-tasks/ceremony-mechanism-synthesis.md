## Cross-mechanism interaction

A departing member's credential can be revoked while a downstream service still has an earlier authorization observation. That creates a conditional enforcement question, not proof of access or harm. Obtaining fresh evidence may itself create a usage-correlation surface; assess status lookup and observer context separately. Preserving evidence of the prior decision may support contestability but does not authorize unlimited retention. If an authoritative resolution arrives, update the particular blocking condition without clearing unrelated requirements. These propositions concern different facts and must not be collapsed into one risk score or counted as independent incidents.
